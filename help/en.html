👋 FaceReg / Reconize — faces and greetings

Open the face program, check that the robot can receive its data, then set what it says and does when it meets someone.

Inside Reconize

1. Open and sign in

In Hub Tools, open Reconize and press Open Reconize. In the new tab, enter your Reconize account and press Login. For a staffed event, use the account your organizer assigned.

2. Check the event dashboard

Open Dashboard. Check registered participants and today’s detections. Use Recent Activity to see whether check-ins are reaching the event.

3. Add and find people

Open People and choose Add Person. Fill in the participant details and add an approved reference photo. Save, then search for the participant and open their record to check it.

4. Import a participant list

Open Import Participants. Choose Upload file for CSV or Excel, or Google Sheet for an approved shared sheet. Download Example shows sample columns. Review the detected name and photo columns before importing. The column preview appears after you choose a file or sheet.

5. Set up check-in stations

Open Activities, type a name and press Add. Choose the camera and mode for that activity, then press Open station. Each station opens in its own window.

6. Use the check-in screen

Open Face Recognition and check Mode. In tap mode, ask the guest to tap TAP TO SCAN, allow the camera if prompted, and follow the consent and check-in prompts shown on the screen.

7. Register a camera station

Open CCTV on the camera computer. Give the station a unique name, select its activity and recognition method, and choose Register this station. Follow the options shown for that station.

8. Open Reconize from another device

Open Connect. On a device on the same Wi-Fi, scan the QR code from your own screen. If Reconize says it is not shared yet, ask the event organizer to start the network version on the host PC.

9. Check who attended

Open Attendees after a check-in. Compare the detected count with the registered total and check the participant’s row before correcting a missing entry.

10. Check submitted photos

Open Upload History to find photos submitted for recognition. If a submission is missing, check that the upload finished on the original device.

11. Find a recognition attempt

Open Recognition History. Search by name, ID or filename and filter by result. Use Export CSV if you need an authorized copy of the records.

12. Review event totals

Open Reports. Compare attendance by activity and check the detection timeline. Choose Export Participants or Export Attendance for the list you need.

13. Check photo consent

Open PDPA and search for the guest. Check their latest consent status before using photos. The status can change after registration if the guest answers at the kiosk.

14. Process event photos

Open Event Photos, then New Batch. Choose the photographer’s authorized source folder and follow the batch instructions. Connect Google Drive only if you want an optional copy in your own Drive.

15. Open the video experiment

In Local Video Experiment, choose a demo video or an approved Drive batch. Upload the video, select it and start the experiment. Review the sampled-frame counts in that run. This screen is ready for a first video. Pick a file to make Upload video available.

16. Check event and camera settings

Open Settings. Check the event name, date and place, then choose the kiosk mode and camera. Save and run a check-in with approved test data before guests arrive. Scroll down to Save Settings.

17. Read the privacy information

From the sign-in screen, open Privacy Policy. Check what the app stores, how long it keeps the data and who handles deletion requests. Scroll down to the sections on retention and deletion.

If Reconize does not open

Return to the Reconize tile in Hub and choose Check it is running, then Open Reconize. If the camera stays black, allow camera access in the browser and close other tabs or apps using it.

Link recognition to Voice

In Hub’s Reconize page, start following Reconize and check the status. Open What the rig says, turn greeting on, choose the speaker and save. In Voice, try a greeting with an authorized test participant.

in What the rig says Open Greet people the face app sees then choose Speaker for where the sound should come out

the box Polite word before a name is the word placed before a name, and If the rig is already talking or playing Choose skip this hello to skip it, or wait its turn to wait

2. Write the greetings and set the pause between repeat greetings

Set the times in Greet the same person at most once every in minutes, and the time for someone not yet known in seconds per camera

In the section When they arrive and When they leave put the words for someone coming the first time, someone coming back, and someone not yet known.

{title} in place of the title, and {name} in place of the name. If someone isn't recognized yet and has no name, use a general greeting such as "Hello, welcome!"

3. Choose the behaviour and moves of each camera

In the section Cameras choose whether that camera greets when it sees someone (says hello), says goodbye (says goodbye) or only counts people (only counts)

Choose Move and the robot in the box on of that camera. The list of moves comes from the files really saved. If you want speech only, choose none

If the camera does not appear yet, use Add a camera by name The camera name must match the data the face program sends.

Save and check the result

Press Save at the bottom and wait for the message confirming it was saved. A change takes effect the next time the system meets someone who matches the conditions. Undo my changes Loads the saved values back.
